01About

From the BEAM to the Linux kernel.

I'm a software engineer with more than a decade of building backends, most of it on real-time messaging and distributed systems. I started on the Microsoft stack with C# and ASP.NET, worked with Go and Node.js, then spent five years on the BEAM writing Erlang and Elixir.

Today I work as a DevOps engineer at Simanban. I run CI/CD and Kubernetes clusters, practice GitOps with ArgoCD, provision virtual machines on Proxmox and VMware with Terraform and Ansible, and keep an eye on everything with Prometheus and the ELK stack.
