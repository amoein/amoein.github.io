01About

From the BEAM to the Linux kernel.

I'm a software engineer with more than a decade of building backends, most of it on real-time messaging and distributed systems. I started on the Microsoft stack with C# and ASP.NET, worked with Go and Node.js, then spent five years on the BEAM writing Erlang and Elixir.

At BisPhone and Pars Telecom I worked on messengers serving large numbers of live, concurrent connections with Erlang, Ejabberd and Cassandra. Remote Elixir work followed: OQTO, a platform linking partners to the finance engine at ClubCollect, the chat platform behind hubber.live, and real-time LiveView dashboards at Marko.

Today I work as a DevOps engineer at Simanban. I run CI/CD and Kubernetes clusters, practice GitOps with ArgoCD, provision virtual machines on Proxmox and VMware with Terraform and Ansible, and keep an eye on everything with Prometheus and the ELK stack. I write up what I learn as practical guides.

I hold a master's and a bachelor's in software engineering, and alongside my day job I've mentored early-stage teams at Sharif Accelerator and Avatech.

03Open source

Small, sharp tools for concurrent systems.

Libraries and experiments for the BEAM, the virtual machine behind Erlang and Elixir.

All repositories
  • amoein/cessna2019

    cessna

    Erlang socket acceptor

    A lightweight TCP acceptor pool for Erlang/OTP. Built for servers that hold live connections, like chat or AMQP, and must survive a flood of simultaneous reconnects after a restart or deploy.

    Erlang
    Example Source
  • amoein/linn2019

    linn

    Erlang ring buffer and process pool

    A process pool manager for Erlang/OTP. It keeps a pool of processes instead of connections, spreads requests across them and handles back pressure, which makes it a good fit for IO-bound work.

    Erlang
    Example Source
  • amoein/elf2023

    elf

    Efficient unique sorted IDs

    A small Erlang library for generating IDs that are unique and sortable.

    Erlang
    Source

    balloon

    Minimal video streaming

    A minimal video streaming experiment built with Elixir and Phoenix LiveView.
